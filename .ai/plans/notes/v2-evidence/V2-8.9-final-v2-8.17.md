## Findings
| id | sev | file:line | chain | fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | medium | sync/offline-overlap.ts:132-163; sync/projection.ts:491-494, 509-533 | **A stale episode survives a disconnect, and the shadow replays updates that causally depend on our own edit.** Nothing ends an episode on a socket drop; only `unsyncedChanges===0` does. Scenario: our update reaches the hub and is applied, then the socket drops before `SyncStatus` arrives (lid close, Wi-Fi roam, or a hub or network-positioned party withholding the downstream frame). An online teammate edits on top of our text. On reconnect the hub's sync-step-2 arrives before the new ack, so `remote()` replays the teammate's update onto `baseState`, which lacks our items. Yjs parks the dependent structs/deletes as pending, so `theirs` is a body nobody authored (e.g. the line they retyped is missing). `mergeSource` fails, and `surfaceOverlap` `applyHtmlToDoc`s that mangled body over the project from this client and stamps the edit as ours. The project then loses our already-accepted edit **and** the teammate's dependent work. `incoming.tsx` saves the mangled text, and the notice says "the project kept theirs", which is false. The same drop also breaks "exactly one side acts": if the teammate was offline too, both sides act and each overwrites with the other's version. No attacker-unique gain (a legacy hub can already rewrite the doc), but this is an integrity defect with a benign trigger, and it reintroduces the very garbling #121 exists to fix, now as a whole-body write. | After each replay, treat `shadow.store.pendingStructs` / `pendingDs` as "cannot tell" and `end()`: a remote update taken *before* ours cannot depend on ours, so pending state proves the invariant broke. Also end, or re-validate, the episode on provider disconnect/re-probe, e.g. drop it when the hub's sync-step-1 state vector already covers our client clock at episode open. Add the drop-after-apply-before-ack lane to `sync-offline-overlap.test.ts`. |
| L1 | low | sync/offline-overlap.ts:160 | A **budget** failure counts as an overlap: `if (mergeSource(...).ok) return null` treats `reason:'budget'` (maxEditLength 4096 / 50 ms diff timeout, source-merge.ts:30) like touching edits. A peer whose concurrent edit is large but does **not** touch ours (or our own large paste meeting any teammate keystroke) still forces `surfaceOverlap`. Our non-touching edit is pulled from the project, and the notice falsely says "you both changed the same part". The disk keeps ours and the next save restores it, hence low. | Act only on a genuine touch: distinguish `reason==='budget'` and, for budget, either leave the result to the CRDT or surface it as `merge-budget` with that copy. |
| L2 | low (pre-existing) | sync/source-recovery.ts:93-103; sync/atomic-write.ts:46-50 | `mkdirSync(recursive)` plus `rename` follow a directory symlink at `_history/<slug>` or `.../sync-recovery`. A repo collaborator who commits such a symlink (git checks it out despite `.gitignore`) redirects recovery writes, peer-controlled bytes under a fixed filename, into any directory. Example: another project's `.design/`, where `sync-recovery/incoming.tsx` becomes a live canvas that syncs to that project's hub. Hub and peers cannot plant it. V2-8.17 only adds one more caller. | Check the realpath of `historyDir` against the realpath of the design root before writing, or `lstat` each segment and refuse symlinks (fail closed with `snapshotFailed`). |
| L3 | low | sync/offline-overlap.ts:94-95, 115-125, 155-160; projection.ts:491-494 | CPU on the server's event loop: each remote transaction in an open episode runs `htmlFromDoc(shadow)` plus `mergeSource` (two diffs, up to 50 ms each). Up to 512 per episode is about 50 s of stall per canvas, and each episode opens with a full `encodeStateAsUpdate` copy, which tombstone bloat inflates. With an honest hub the window is one RTT. It is long only in the M1 stale-episode case, or when a malicious hub withholds acks. | Lower `MAX_EPISODE_UPDATES` or rate-limit `check()` (debounce to the last remote update before the ack, or check only on step-2/batched). Cap `baseState` bytes and drop the episode past the cap. |

No viable chain promotes any finding to high. M1 does the most damage, but it needs a drop-before-ack window and an attacker gains nothing a legacy-mode hub doesn't already have. L1 + L2 do not compose: the L1 write lands in a fixed slot, and L2 needs a repo-level plant.
