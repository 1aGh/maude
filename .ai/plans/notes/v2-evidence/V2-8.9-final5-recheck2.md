- **Chain, reachable on the hub only:**
  1. The checkout is a tenant-controlled clone (DDR-054, `asset-lane.mjs:444`). A tenant who can push commits a real `link/foo.css`, and it gets journaled.
  2. They then replace `link/` with a symlink to any folder on the host.

- **Leak:** one bit per probe — does `<stem>.tsx` or `<stem>.meta.json` exist in a host folder the tenant chooses. Nothing is read.
