## Root causes (several artboards)

1. **`empty-copy.js` `fill()` has no plural handling.** `{count} chats`, `{count} artboards`, `Restore {count} artboards`, `Searched {count} canvases/assets` all read wrong at 1. CONTRACT §4 forbids that, and A20 keeps English-only, so a small `n === 1 ? noun : nouns` slot type would fix it. Hits es-edge-panels (seen), es-arriving, es-all-trashed, es-search-*.
2. **Surfaces bypass the copy table.** The Home placeholder (`HomeAsk.jsx:15`), the AI-chat empty (`Suggest.jsx:22`) and the Home starters (`home-starters.js`) leave `empty-copy.js` keys `es-home-*.placeholder`, `es-home-*.starter-*` and `es-panels-make.ai-none` dead.
3. **Other UI pre-empts the empty state.** The v1-style version row on a new canvas ("Changed this canvas · changed outside the app"), the generic canvas line over an all-trashed canvas, the generic Assets empty over a no-hit search, and "Recent canvases" above the Home shelf.
4. **The Connect sheet hosted in the AI panel** clips CONTRACT §7's Connect / Cancel (shared with 02).
5. **DOM tag names used as Layers names** (`canvas-shell.tsx:439-449`), shared with 01.
6. **States that need a hub or a second person** (es-viewer, es-all-trashed, the team-of-one sheet, es-arriving skeletons) are still unverifiable. Their strings exist in the table, but no lane can render them.
