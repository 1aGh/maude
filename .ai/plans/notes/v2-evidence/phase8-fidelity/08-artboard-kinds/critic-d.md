## Root causes still open
1. Picker still draws the phone for App/Social and the camera for Video; search autofocus ring, rhythm, placement (`SizePicker.jsx`, `size-picker.css`); Custom size replaces the picker body instead of an island beside it.
2. Artboard inspector slots are still generic: Fill is a "Background" chip row under a section head, Layout carries Padding/Gap on web, safe-zone notes sit in the card, Paper repeats the size, print fields have a grip.
3. Platform list for safe zones is a select; the floating checked menu is not built.
4. Canvases chip still dims the canvas (shared kind store) and rows have no thumbnails; Layers still prints raw DOM children; kind-filter states (Print chip dim, "Show all") are not driven.
5. Video timeline head/track heads/poster flag are the 07 timeline, not the board's compact one; the video inspector does not open with the timeline up.
6. Convert flow: body stuck on "Checking…", after-state selects the post (not the A6 copy) and the toast says "artboard" not "post".
7. Warnings: no on-canvas "crosses the cut" mark; only one of the board's three cases is shootable.
8. Harness gaps leave ak-web-widths, ak-print-view menu, ak-mixed-select and ak-advanced unconfirmed.
