## Open (and why)

- **SkeletonArtboard (05 es-arriving) still has no caller.** The artboards of a canvas that is on its way are not known on this Mac: `/_api/sync/availability` lists `{path, name, state}` only, never the artboards' names and sizes. A real mount needs the sync manifest to carry them (server + hub), not a client change.
- **Version history "Versions appear here as you work."** (05 es-panels-canvas): the row the impl shows is the fixture — the canvas file was written outside the app, so the history honestly says "changed outside the app". A canvas made in the app has no such row.
- **Comments panel left vs right and the "All" filter** — decided (`v2-8.5-c2-contract-over-board-comments`): right, four filters. Not re-litigated.
- **@ picker in the harness** has no people to list (no peers), so the picker frame cannot be shot; its anatomy is pinned by the CSS tests.
- **co-comment-video timeline strip / print guides / existing Jonas thread with Resolve** (10): states need a second person's comments and a video comp with markers; not reachable in the lane.
- **Reachability evidence** (`reachability.json`): the renamed hook's key was edited by hand (same element, same evidence); `canvases-ds-make` now needs the Advanced fold open — a `probe-reach.mjs` re-run is the gate's (Playwright's browser cache is missing on this machine, `playwright install` is a download).
- **Four kept targets still open in `check-v2-nothing-deleted`** (`switcher-open-folder`, `comment-composer-cancel/-place/-send`): open on the feat tip before this round; the probe's composer opener does not reach them.
