## Manual contrast sweep (the owed item)

Floating panels (left panel, inspector, AI chat, toolbars, menus, sheets, palette) are **solid by design** (`--island-bg`
= `--bg-1`, `--island-backdrop: none`, V2-1.8 M1; popovers/sheets opaque): the text behind them cannot change with what
sits under the island. Measured anyway, not assumed: a "Busy" canvas (a plasma photo, a pure black and a pure white
artboard, zoom-to-fit) in both themes; for each state every text element outside artboards had its ink read from its
computed colour (alpha and opacity applied), the text hidden, a screenshot taken, and the ink compared to the pixels
actually behind it (median over the glyph box, large-text threshold 3:1). 13 states × 2 themes (base, artboard
selected ×2, project menu, More menu, shape tool, Present options, Share, Export, palette, comments, Assets, Layers, AI
chat ± typed text), 40–106 text elements each. Result: the two real failures above (#9, #10) plus nothing in the HUD that
is not explained by overlap: on-canvas chips (selection tag, size tag, peer cursor and selection labels) are solid pills
and held 4.5:1. axe's "incomplete" groups (≈50 distinct selectors: kind-chip counts, kbd hints, `.dc-annot-text`, artboard
labels, empty-state copy over a gradient) were each checked in the same sweep or a screenshot; none other read low.
Noted, not a contrast failure: when a collaborator has an artboard selected, their selection label sits on top of that
artboard's name label (the name is covered) — see Moderates.

## Moderates (not fixed) with a recommendation

* `.chat-feed` (AI chat) is a focusable `div` with `aria-label="Conversation"` and no role — axe `aria-prohibited-attr`
  (reported as "incomplete", serious-class, needs review). Recommend `role="log"` (it is a message feed; it brings an
  implicit polite live region, so check double announcements with VoiceOver first) or `role="region"`. Needs a call.
* A peer's selection label covers the artboard's own name label when both are on the same artboard. Recommend offsetting
  the peer label above the name, or merging them.
* `landmark-one-main` / `page-has-heading-one` on the tab strip, `index.html`, `root-missing.html`; `landmark-unique`
  ("Layout") with Settings over the inspector; `bypass` (iframe) — unchanged from the earlier record, left.
* A Tab from "Open Inspector" reaches a toaster wrapper before leaving the document (focus lands on `body`); harmless.
