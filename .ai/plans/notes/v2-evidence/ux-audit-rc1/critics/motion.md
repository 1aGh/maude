Evidence gaps, with no finding:
- **AI chat.** The AI chat never appears in `07-ai-chat/01`–`06`, so the chat's open, collapse and resize motion can't be judged. Michal's #9 and #11 already cover Advanced and collapse.
- **What's new duration.** What's new stays visible across most shots, up to 8 min into a session (`07-ai-chat/01-chat-open.png`). The code's 10 s timer acknowledges and persists the toast (`notifications.tsx:226`), so the lead should confirm on the `.app` that it really expires before treating this as a bug.
- **Reduced motion is otherwise sound.** The `--dur-*` tokens collapse to 1 ms, fold ghosts are skipped, and toasts get `animation: none`.
- **Inspector rules.** No disagreement with IR-1..17.

## Taste decisions

- **T1 · The camera on a mode crossing (M8).**
  - (a) Keep the ease. This is the recorded design.
  - (b) Keep the design still while the panels fold over it, and ease only if the artboard you were on would end up hidden.
  - (c) Ease only when the person clicks the Preview button, and stay still on key crossings (N C M A E S, Esc).
  - **Pick (b).** A mode switch is a context change, and the design is the subject, so it shouldn't move. (c) is the smaller change if (b) is too big for rc.
- **T2 · The Canvases fold on selection (M3).**
  - (a) Keep it, with hysteresis: restore only after about 600 ms with no new pick.
  - (b) Fold only when the Inspector and Canvases islands wouldn't both fit. At 1600 px they do fit (264 + 264 + gaps), so nothing moves.
  - (c) Never fold.
  - **Pick (b), plus (a)'s hysteresis below the threshold.**
- **T3 · The first-run toast order (M1).**
  - (a) What's new first, then the mode hint after it leaves.
  - (b) The mode hint first, because it's about what you're doing now. What's new waits, or moves to the Help badge only.
  - (c) Merge them into one pill: "You're in Edit · What's new (3)".
  - **Pick (b).** Task guidance is primary and product news is tertiary.
- **T4 · The Edit → Preview choreography (M7).**
  - (a) Everything at once in 220 ms, as now.
  - (b) Staggered at 0 / 40 / 120 ms, about 340 ms in total.
  - (c) One quick 120 ms cross-fade for everything.
  - **Pick (b).**
