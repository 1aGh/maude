## Taste decisions (Michal)

- **T1 · Name of the annotation mode** (CONTRACT §Preview is FigJam-style annotation):
  (a) keep "Preview"; (b) rename it **"Review"**: it's where comments, stickies and stamps live, and it stops reading as
  "see the prototype"; (c) split it into "Preview" (Hand + live design only) and "Annotate" (the tools), four modes.
  **Pick (b).** It's one word, fits what the mode does, and adds no mode.
- **T2 · Annotation keys pressed in Edit** (they switch modes now):
  (a) keep the switch, plus a persistent mode label and a toast that names the switch; (b) N/C work inside Edit with no mode change, and the other keys switch;
  (c) the first press asks once ("Stickies live in Review. Switch?"), then it's silent.
  **Pick (a).** It keeps the contract and makes the switch visible, and that alone removes the "where did my Edit go" moment.
- **T3 · What "New canvas" opens:**
  (a) an empty canvas with the size picker open; (b) the brief board, as now; (c) a two-choice row: "Empty" / "From a brief".
  **Pick (a)**, with "Start from a brief" in the empty-artboard hint. Figma people expect a blank canvas, and the brief is one click away.
- **T4 · Default look of a new shape or frame:**
  (a) a neutral grey token fill / surface fill on frames; (b) the system accent; (c) currentColor (the #8 direction).
  **Pick (a).** The accent is a signal (UX-ACT-2), and currentColor in dark chrome can come out as near-invisible black.
- **T5 · Mode switch:**
  (a) a labelled segmented control "Edit · Preview · Present"; (b) icons only, labels on hover (as now); (c) one "Edit ▾" pill.
  **Pick (a).** Equal cells (UX-HIER-7), and the mode is always readable.

On the Inspector rules (IR-1..17): I agree with them. The one addition from this lens is U02's: a fresh object is
never invisible.
