## On the Inspector rules (IR-1..17, O1–O4)

- I agree overall. **O1 a** (calm) and **O2 a** (popover from the chip) are also my picks, on two conditions: the popover is the top layer (above toasts, GD-2), and it is collision-aware (GD-4).
- **IR-16:** add "a value without a control sits in the control column as fg-3 text" (GD-13), and "Advanced uses the same label column" (GD-15).
- **Missing:** a header rule, "the object is named once: crumb + kind chip, no Kind row" (GD-11), and a prose rule, "at most one fg-3 line per section; explanations behind ?" (GD-12).

## Taste decisions

- **T1 · Accent rationing (GD-27).**
  - a: accent only on the primary action and the selection; tabs and segments use a neutral raised surface.
  - **b ★: accent on the primary action, the selection and one active state per control group; tags, chips, links and "kept open" go neutral.**
  - c: keep as is.
  - My pick is b because UX-NAV-1 allows the accent on an active state, but one per group.
- **T2 · Edit mode dims content (GD-7).**
  - **a ★: content renders identically in Edit and Preview.**
  - b: dim only annotations (stickies, notes) in Edit, behind a View toggle.
  - c: keep as is.
- **T3 · The frame on Edit → Preview (GD-6).**
  - **a ★: the same frame; only the tool bar's contents and the toggle change.**
  - b: Preview fades the chrome out but keeps every position, with no camera move.
  - c: keep as is.
- **T4 · Where "What's new" lives (GD-1, GD-2).**
  - **a ★: the Home line plus the Help › What's new badge only; never a canvas toast.**
  - b: one toast per version that dismisses itself after about 8 s.
  - c: keep the persistent toast.
- **T5 · Menu vs panel elevation (GD-5).**
  - **a ★: menus one surface step lighter, plus a 1 px border, in both themes.**
  - b: a stronger shadow only.
  - c: keep the surface and offset menus so they never share a panel edge.
- **T6 · Explanations in the Inspector (GD-12).**
  - **a ★: behind a "?" on the section title or a tooltip.**
  - b: at most one fg-3 line per section.
  - c: keep as is.
