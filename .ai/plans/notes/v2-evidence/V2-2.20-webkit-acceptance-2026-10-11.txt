Not V2-2.20 scope (decision v2-2.20-fit-all-raster-cost, V2-8.8): the ABSOLUTE cost — ~1 s zoom
frames and ~44 long frames at fit-all with 21 boards at 2× — is the same in both sizes, i.e. today's
behaviour. New evidence for that finding: ~1800 DCArtboard renders per 100-frame gesture. Scout
(code reading, not measured): the gesture is meant to be render-free, but frames > 220 ms end the
interact burst on every wheel event (markInteracting's 220 ms timer, canvas-lib.tsx ~1210–1245),
which flips isInteracting + publishes the viewport; the unmemoised controller object
(useViewportController ~1716–1727) re-renders all 21 boards through ControllerContext. A symptom of
the slow raster that also feeds it. Candidate fixes (not done): keep isInteracting out of React
(ref + data attribute), memoise/split the controller context, adaptive debounce.
