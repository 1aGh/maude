**The first <CloudPrice of="trialDays" /> days are free.** If you start through checkout, your card is saved and checked but not charged. After the trial the plan renews automatically until you cancel, and you can stop any time before the trial ends. If you start a free project from the desktop app, no card is needed. We also do not charge for a project that failed to come up: if provisioning does not finish, the card is not charged. That ordering is enforced in code, not by intention.

You may also withdraw within 14 days as a consumer. See [Withdrawal and refunds](/docs/legal/withdrawal).

## What you also need, which we do not sell you

Being told this after entering a card would be a bad way to find out, so:

- a **desktop computer** — macOS or Windows, not a phone or a tablet;
- the **free Maude desktop app** ([maude.sh/desktop](/desktop));
- for AI-assisted design, **your own Claude subscription** — about €20 a month, paid to Anthropic on their terms. We do not resell it and receive no part of it.

That third item is also the reason your work stays private: Maude Cloud stores, syncs and versions your project, but your designs are only ever rendered on your own machine. The mechanism behind that claim is on the [Trust](/docs/cloud/trust) page.

## What happens when you stop paying

Nothing sudden, and nothing silent.

1. A failed payment leaves the project **running** while we retry and contact you.
2. After the grace period it **pauses**. Pausing stops the workspace; it deletes nothing.
3. **A complete copy of your work is prepared and emailed to you before any teardown.** The system cannot reach a deleted state except through that step.
4. The data is kept **30 days** after the pause. You are emailed again two days before it goes.
5. Restarting at any point before that brings everything back — same address, same people.

You can download everything at any time, including while a project is paused, and you can delete a project yourself whenever you like. Deleting erases the stored data, including the copies prepared on the download page — so take the one you want first.

## The free alternative

Maude is **free forever to self-host**, and it is the same software rather than a reduced edition: `maude hub workspace-up` stands up the identical stack on your own box, and the [self-hosting cost table](/docs/hub/pricing) is published too.

That belongs on a pricing page rather than buried: a hosted service you cannot leave is a hosted service whose promises cannot be tested.
