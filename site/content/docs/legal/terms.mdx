## 2. What the service is

A **project** is one hosted workspace with its own address, its own people, and its own design system. It stores and synchronises your design files and their history, and it hands them back on demand.

**It does not render your designs.** Rendering happens on your own computer, in the Maude desktop app. This is a technical property of the service, not a policy — see [Trust](/docs/cloud/trust) for the mechanisms.

### What you need, and what it costs you elsewhere

Using Maude Cloud requires things we do not sell you:

- a **desktop computer** (macOS or Windows) — not a phone or a tablet;
- the **free Maude desktop app**;
- for AI-assisted design, **your own Claude subscription**, purchased from Anthropic. It is billed by Anthropic, on their terms, and we neither resell it nor receive any part of it.

We state this before you reach a payment form. If you did not want it stated, the trial is how you find out cheaply.

## 5. If a payment fails

A failed payment is a conversation, not a switch:

1. The project **keeps running** while we retry and contact you.
2. If it is still unpaid after the grace period stated on your billing page, the project **pauses**. Pausing stops the workspace; it does not delete anything.
3. **Before any teardown, a complete copy of your work is prepared and offered to you.** The service cannot reach a deleted state except through that step — it is a state machine, not a promise.
4. Data is retained for the period stated on the billing and cancellation screens, and the countdown is shown to you on screen and by email before it runs out.

## 6. Cancelling and leaving

You can cancel at any time from your project's billing page. You can also cancel at link.com, where the seller of record manages your subscription. The screen tells you, before you click: the date the project keeps working until, the date it pauses, and the date the data is deleted.

You can **download everything at any time**, including while a project is paused or past due. The copy is yours to keep forever, and taking it changes nothing about your project.

You can delete a project yourself. Deletion is only offered once a complete copy exists, and when you confirm it we stop billing, detach the address, and **erase the stored data**.

### Refunds

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not refund the period you have already paid for, and we do not refund an annual plan pro rata when you cancel part-way through. The project keeps working until the end of the period. The exception is the consumer right of withdrawal below, which is a legal right and always applies. If a payment was taken in error, or the service did not work for you, write to **cloud@maude.sh** and we will look at it fairly.

## If you are a consumer: withdrawal [#withdrawal]

If you buy as a consumer in the EU, you may withdraw from the contract within **14 days** of concluding it, without giving a reason.

- **If you started with a free trial**, the trial covers that period. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.
- **If you chose a plan that starts immediately**, you asked us to start the service before the 14 days end. If you withdraw, we refund what you paid minus a proportionate amount for the days you used.

To withdraw, send us a clear statement, for example using the [model withdrawal form](/docs/legal/withdrawal), to **cloud@maude.sh**. We confirm by email and refund within 14 days through the original payment method. See also [Withdrawal and refunds](/docs/legal/withdrawal).
