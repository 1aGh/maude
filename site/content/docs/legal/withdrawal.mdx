## Your right to withdraw

If you buy Maude Cloud as a consumer in the EU, you may withdraw from the contract within **14 days** of concluding it, without giving a reason. The full wording is in the [Terms](/docs/legal/terms#withdrawal).

- **Started with a free trial:** the trial covers the 14 days. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.
- **Chose a plan that starts immediately:** you asked us to start the service before the 14 days end. If you withdraw, we refund what you paid minus a proportionate amount for the days you used.

To withdraw, send us a clear statement, for example the form below, to **cloud@maude.sh**. We confirm by email and refund within 14 days through the original payment method.

## Refunds

Outside the right of withdrawal, cancelling stops the next renewal and does not refund the period already paid. The rest is in [section 6 of the Terms](/docs/legal/terms#refunds).
